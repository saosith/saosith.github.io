## Introduction

Each of the four LEDs is one bit of a binary number, and the circuit steps through a
set sequence of numbers on repeat. I built it on a breadboard from 74HC logic chips and
a 555 timer. The blue Elegoo board in the photo only supplies power from my laptop.

## Architecture and components

The 555 timer runs in astable mode, so it keeps switching on its own and each pulse is
one clock tick.

Four 74HC D flip-flops hold the current number, one per LED. 74HC AND, OR and NOT
gates read that number and work out the next one. On
each tick the flip-flops load it and the LEDs change.
