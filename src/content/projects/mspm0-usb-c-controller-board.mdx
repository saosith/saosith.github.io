## Introduction

One microcontroller board I can reuse across projects, powered and debugged from my
laptop.

## Architecture and components

- **MSPM0G3507:** Arm Cortex-M0+ microcontroller
- **USB-C + 5.1 kΩ CC resistors:** 5 V power from a laptop or charger
- **SPX3819 LDO:** 5 V to 3.3 V
- **USBLC6-2SC6:** ESD protection on D+ and D−
- **CH340E:** USB-to-UART, shows up on the PC as a serial port
- **8 MHz crystal:** accurate UART timing
- **RC filter:** clean ADC reference
- **TC2030 Tag-Connect:** SWD programming with no header

## Requirements

- USB-C power from a laptop
- Serial link to a PC, no adapter
- Programming with no header
- Clean ADC reference
