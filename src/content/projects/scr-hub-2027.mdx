## Introduction

Every board on our robot plugs into the Hub for 12 V power, the CAN bus and the E-Stop
line. For 2027 the team also wanted to see how much current each board draws, so the
Hub measures current and voltage on every port. I did the schematic, part selection and
layout.

## Architecture and components

- **XT60, 10 A fuse, SMBJ15A TVS:** battery input and surge protection
- **DMP4015SK3Q-13 P-MOSFET:** reverse-polarity protection
- **AP63301 buck converter:** 12 V to 3.3 V for the chips on the board
- **INA220 + 20 mΩ shunt (x7):** current and voltage on each port, over I²C
- **STM32C092 + MAX3051:** microcontroller and CAN transceiver, 8 MHz crystal
- **SN74AHC244:** copies the E-Stop line to every port, no firmware in the path
- **CH340C + USB-C:** USB-to-UART debugging
- **6-pin Molex (x7):** board ports, each with its own fuse and status LED

## Requirements

- Power, CAN and E-Stop to every board through 6-pin Molex connectors
- Current and voltage sensing per board
- 5-8 A total, not counting the motors
- Hardware-only E-Stop path
- Fuse, then TVS, then reverse protection at the input
- 4 layers, 100 x 100 mm max
- Hand-solderable SMD parts from DigiKey or LCSC
